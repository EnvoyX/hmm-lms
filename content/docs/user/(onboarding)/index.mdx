# Welcome to HMM LMS Docs

Welcome! This documentation platform is your central hub for navigating, managing, and getting the most out of the **HMM LMS** platform. Whether you are a student, instructor, or administrator, these guides are designed to help you quickly understand workflows and features.

<Callout type="info" title="Quick Tip">

## What You Can Find Here

This documentation is split into tailored sections depending on your role and goals:

* **User Guides:** Learn how to access courses, submit assignments, track learning progress, and manage your account settings.
* **Walkthroughs & FAQs:** Step-by-step solutions to common account issues, enrollment questions, and platform navigation.
